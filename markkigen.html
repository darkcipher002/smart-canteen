Customers automatically earn 1 point for every KES 100 spent on completed orders. Bronze (0+), Silver (100+), Gold (300+), Platinum (700+). You can also add or remove points manually below.

Danger Zone

These actions permanently delete data from your database. There is no undo.

Delete all Cancelled orders

Clears out rejected/cancelled orders to declutter your history.

Clears completed order history. Loyalty points already earned are not affected.

Removes every dish from your menu. Customers will see an empty menu until you add new items.
